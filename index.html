About me

I’ve always believed that good design is experienced, not just seen.

I care about how something works just as much as how it looks. Every spacing decision, every interaction, every word on screen matters. Attention to detail isn’t a habit, it’s how I think.

My process starts before any screen is designed. I research the problem, map user flows and challenge assumptions early, using Claude to prototype and stress-test ideas and Figma to bring them to life with precision.
